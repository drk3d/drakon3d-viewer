Privacy Notice

Your models stay under your control.

Effective: September 4, 2026

What this notice covers

This notice explains how Drakon3D Viewer handles information when you use the viewer at viewer.drakon3d.com. Drakon3D Viewer is a browser-based tool for opening and viewing 3D model files.

Model files and viewer data

Models that you open from your device are processed in your browser. The viewer does not operate an application server that receives, stores, or sells your model files, measurements, annotations, or viewing sessions.

Viewer preferences, such as theme, language, named views, and saved local session data, may be stored in your browser's local storage or IndexedDB. You can remove this data by clearing this site's browser storage.

Optional cloud connections

Cloud connections are optional and start only after you choose a provider and sign in. Each provider presents its own consent screen and privacy terms.

  • Google Drive: the viewer asks for access to files you select or use with the viewer through Google's drive.file permission. A short-lived access token is held in browser memory while needed to open the chosen file.
  • OneDrive: the viewer uses Microsoft Graph delegated access to list your drive and read a file that you choose. Microsoft sign-in information is cached locally by the Microsoft sign-in library so that your browser can restore your session.
  • Dropbox: the Dropbox Chooser lets you select a file from your own Dropbox account and supplies the viewer with a temporary link for that selection.

Drakon3D Viewer does not receive your cloud-provider password. You can revoke the viewer's cloud access at any time in the security settings of the relevant provider.

Hosting and third parties

The viewer is delivered through GitHub Pages. That host and the cloud providers named above may process technical information, such as IP address, browser details, and service logs, under their own privacy policies. Drakon3D Viewer does not use advertising trackers or sell personal information.

Contact and changes

For questions about this notice or the viewer, contact hello@drakon3d.com. If this notice changes materially, the revised version will be published here with an updated effective date.
